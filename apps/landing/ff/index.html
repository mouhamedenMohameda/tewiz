Ko waɗi Aloo

Defi ɗo, fii ngam meeɗen.

Wonaa apliki caggal leydi nde wiɗɗitaa law. Apliki defaa fii Muritani — ɗemɗe maa, wuro maa, no ngonɗaa yoɓirde.

Daande maa yonii

Aɗa winndataa hay huunde. Haal tan ko ɗo njahataa — e pulaar, wolof, soninke, ƴaaliya, farayse, maa aarabe — caɗa fuɗɗoo.

Caɗa wonande gooto neɗɗo, e telefon maa

Neɗɗo on alaa apliki? Yamir wonande mo, ko honto kaalataa. Soforoowo no noddoyamoo ɗiɓa, an yoɓa e konton maa — Anndaa kay.

Yoɓ e kash

No e ɓooyii, e jokkere caɗa. Karta banki alaa, konton internet kadi alaa.

Soforooɓe maa keborɓe

Soforoowo a hooli? Defu mo cuɓɓo — o aray yeeso laawol garotaa.

Caɗe heddoraa ɗii

Laawol gootol kala ñalnde, ekoolu maa gollirgu? Defu ɗum yeru gootol, yejjit caarka subaka.

Neldu pakke

Kayit maa pakke neldotaako? Soforoowo aday salaade ɗum, e jaɓɓirgol e koodu ngaarnde araa.

No nde gollotoo

Caɗa maa adannde, e jaɓɓe tati.

1

Winndan-min e WhatsApp

Min defay konton maa e duuɓi ɗiɗi. Min ƴetta hay neɗɗo kala e jungo — ngam kisal won fof.

2

Haal ko ɗo njahataa

Uddit apliki, haal maa meem karte. Min hebay soforoowo ɓadiiɗo maa e secondes.

3

Wa e ƴeftu

Reɓo ɓattagol e ndeer waktu, teeŋtinir koodu maa nayool, yoɓ kash gasol.

Ko waɗi defugol passir WhatsApp? Ngam kisal maa, konton kala no fellitee e fedde amen — ɗum ko hadata ɓe penooɓe. Ƴetto seconde tan, walaa ko ɓuri.
