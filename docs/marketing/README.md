# Aloo — Marketing

Everything for taking **Aloo** to market in Nouakchott. Start here.

## The strategy in one paragraph

Aloo is a **two-sided marketplace** (riders + captains), so the only thing that matters at launch is **liquidity**: a rider must open the app and see a car nearby. That means **supply first** — recruit a dense wave of captains in **one Nouakchott zone** before spending a dirham on rider ads, then launch demand into real cars. Lead with the three things no foreign app has here: **voice booking in your own language, cash, and "made in Mauritania."** *Aloo* — short, friendly, like picking up the phone — is the brand's gift; lean into warmth, trust, and the "order a ride for a loved one" feature.
