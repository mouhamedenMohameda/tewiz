## 1. What Aloo is

> **Aloo is the Mauritanian ride app you book by voice, in your own language, and pay in cash.**

A ride feels as easy and familiar as **calling someone you trust**. That's the whole idea — and it's in the name: short, friendly, like picking up the phone — *Aloo*.

- **Today:** rides (course / VTC) in Nouakchott.
- **Tomorrow:** packages, restaurants, shops & services — a local everyday app. (Lead with rides; the name and tagline already leave room to grow.)

### Why we win here (lead with these, in order)
1. 🎙️ **Your voice is enough** — *« Ta voix suffit. »* / *« صوتك يكفي »*. Order in **hassaniya, French, Arabic, pulaar, wolof or soninké** — all 6 Mauritanian languages. No typing place names. Our #1 hook.
2. 🇲🇷 **100% Mauritanian** — local place names, made here, not a foreign app.
3. 💵 **Cash** — *« Khalleṣ kash »* / *« خلّص كاش »*. No bank card needed.
4. 👵 **A ride for a loved one, from your phone** — order for someone who doesn't have the app (your mother, a relative). The driver calls her, picks her up; you pay from your account — *she doesn't even know*. The emotional heart of the brand.
5. ⭐ **Your own drivers** (favorites), 📅 recurring rides, 📦 packages.

## 2. The name is the strategy

"Aloo" is your best marketing asset. Use it deliberately:

- **Aloo** → the call / the voice booking / "we pick up, we take care of it."
- **Warmth & trust** → the literal *"order a ride for a loved one"* feature: family, respect for elders, taking care of yours.

Brand one-liner that ties it together: **« Comme appeler un proche. »** *(Like calling someone you love.)*

## 3. Voice & tone

**Warm, local, proud, plain-spoken. Never corporate. Never cold.**

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Speak like a trusted neighbor | Sound like a bank or a telco |
| Short sentences. One idea per post. | Paragraphs of features |
| Mix FR + Arabic + Hassaniya naturally; sprinkle in pulaar/wolof/soninké phrases when posting to those communities | Force one language only |
| Respect elders (use *vous* for/about them) | Be slangy about family or elders |
| Be proud it's Mauritanian | Copy Yango's tone |
| Show real captains, real streets of Nouakchott | Stock photos of foreign cities |

**Pronoun rule:** `tu` for youth, drivers, and playful social posts; `vous` for elders, support replies, and the "ride for a loved one" theme.

**Hassaniya in copy:** lean into it — it's the differentiator no foreign app can fake. Use real Hassaniya verbs (*tkellem*, *wnwasslouk*, *5lleṣ*, *6leb*, *mchwar*, *lwalde*, *lbntk*) in headlines, captions, video subtitles, and driver outreach. Even when posting in French or Arabic, drop one Hassaniya word for warmth.

## 4. Tagline system

| Use | French | Arabic (Hassaniya-flavored) | Hassaniya (Latin) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary (riders)** | **Parle. On t'amène.** | **احكي... ونوصّلوك.** | **tkellem · wnwasslouk** |
| Voice hook (sub-line) | Ta voix suffit. | صوتك يكفي. | sawtak kavi |
| App-in-one-line | L'app de course mauritanienne. Commande à la voix, paie cash. | تطبيق المشاوير الموريتاني. اطلب بصوتك، وخلّص كاش. | t6bi9 coursat · 6leb bsawtak · 5llas kiv b9yt |
| "Ride for a loved one" — headline | Une course pour un proche, depuis ton tel. | مشوار لشخص تحبّه، من تيلفونك. | mchwar llwalde |
| "Ride for a loved one" — proof | Commande pour elle. Toi, tu paies. | اطلب ليها انت. وانت تخلّص من حسابك. | 6leb lha · nte t5allas · hiy ma 3lmet ila b9yt |
| Drivers (recruiting) | Gagne plus. Le reste, dans ta poche. | ربح أكثر. والباقي في جيبك. | rbah aktar · w lba9i f lbntk |
| Emotional / brand | Comme appeler un proche. | المشوار... على بعد مكالمة. | — |
| Essence / super-app | Allô. On s'occupe de tout. | ألو. واحنا نكفّوك. | — |

**Primary line everywhere:** **Parle. On t'amène. / احكي... ونوصّلوك. / tkellem · wnwasslouk.**

Pair it with the voice sub-line (**Ta voix suffit. / صوتك يكفي.**) under the headline whenever there's room. Rotate the rest by context.

**Why Hassaniya in Latin script:** young Mauritanians text and post in latinized Hassaniya every day. Putting *tkellem · wnwasslouk* on a TikTok or a flyer is instantly recognizable in a way that Modern Standard Arabic is not. Treat it as a third tagline language, not a translation.

## 5. Visual system — "Sahara Solaire"

Pulled directly from the app so brand and product match exactly.

### Logo
- **Primary mark:** the geometric **"A" monogram** in warm white on the Sahara gradient — *this is the shipped app icon* (`apps/mobile/assets/icon.png`). Use it as the avatar on every platform.
- **Wordmark lockup:** icon + "Aloo" set in **Sora ExtraBold**, horizontal.
- **Mono marks:** [`logo-mono-ink.svg`](../../apps/mobile/assets/brand/logo-mono-ink.svg) (dark on sand) and `logo-mono-white.svg` (white on photos/dark). Use these for stickers, watermarks, vests.
- **Clear space:** keep at least the height of the "A" bar clear on all sides. Never stretch, recolor, add shadows, or put the gradient icon on a busy photo without a solid tile behind it.
- *v2 idea (not now):* the call-bubble "A" (`concept-a-bubble-icon.svg`) leans into the voice story — revisit after launch.

### Color palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / CTA | **Ember** | `#F2682C` |
| Pressed / deep | Ember Deep | `#D9531B` |
| Secondary | **Sun** (marigold) | `#F6A623` |
| Highlight / gradient stop | Saffron | `#FBC65A` |
| Text / near-black | **Ink** (espresso) | `#2C1D10` |
| Background | **Canvas** (sand) | `#FBF3E7` |
| Cards / warm white | Surface | `#FFFCF6` |
| Dark surface (hero/night) | Espresso | `#2A1A0E` |

**Signature gradient (use for hero tiles & the icon):** `#F6A623 → #F2682C → #D9531B` (golden hour).
**Rule:** warm sand or espresso backgrounds — **never clinical white, never cold gray.**

### Typography
- **Display / headlines / big numbers:** **Sora** (ExtraBold / Bold). Tight letter-spacing.
- **Body / UI / captions:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** (Regular → Bold).
- Both are free Google Fonts (already in the app). Use the same two everywhere — posts, site, print.

### Imagery direction
Real Nouakchott. Real captains and their cars. Golden-hour light. Warm tones. Phones showing the app mid-voice-booking. Avoid generic stock and foreign skylines.
