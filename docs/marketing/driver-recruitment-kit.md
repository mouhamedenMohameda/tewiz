# Aloo — Driver (Captain) Recruitment Kit

> The supply engine. Riders are easy; **drivers are the hard, decisive side.** Get this right and the launch works. Get it wrong and riders open the app to no cars and never return.
> Pairs with Week 1 of the [14-day sprint](./launch-14-day-sprint.md). Customer-facing copy is in FR + Arabic; internal playbook is in English.

---

## 1. The goal & the math

**Target:** enough active captains in ONE Nouakchott zone (Tevragh Zeina + Ksar + Capitale + airport run) that a rider sees a car **< 7 min away** at peak. First wave: **30–60 verified, online captains.**

Recruitment is a funnel that leaks at every step, so work backwards:

| Stage | Typical conversion | To get 40 online captains |
|---|---|---|
| Drivers you contact | — | **~250–300** |
| → Interested (reply / say yes) | ~35% | ~95 |
| → Submit documents (KYC) | ~60% | ~57 |
| → Pass KYC & go online | ~70% | **~40** |

So **plan to reach ~250–300 drivers in 10 days** — ~25–30 conversations a day. That's very doable with the channels below + referral.

---

## 2. Founding Captains — your cold-start hook

A driver's #1 objection is *"there aren't enough riders yet."* You can't beat it with promises — beat it with a **founding-driver deal** that makes joining early the smart move:

- 🏅 **"Capitaine Fondateur"** status (first 50). A badge in-app + bragging rights.
- 💸 **Zero commission for the first 2–4 weeks** (or a guaranteed-earnings floor during soft launch).
- 🎁 **Best referral bonus** reserved for founders (see §6).
- 🗣️ A real voice in the product — their feedback shapes v1.
- 🥇 Priority dispatch & favorite-driver head start.

> Set the exact terms (free weeks vs. earnings guarantee) before you recruit — it's the single most persuasive line in every script.

---

## 3. The economics (your strongest pitch — lead with money)

Drivers care about one thing first: **how much do I keep?** Your numbers are genuinely great — use them.

- **You keep ~93% of every ride.** Aloo's commission is **7%** (10% for colis) — *far* below what foreign apps take here. Make the comparison explicit: on a 200 MRU course, Aloo takes 14 MRU; the rider pays you cash, you keep the rest.
- **Cash, every day.** No waiting for a weekly payout — you're paid in hand at the end of each course.
- **Simple wallet.** You pre-load a small commission balance via **Bankily / Masrivi** (send a screenshot, the team credits you). Each ride just deducts its small commission. You can keep driving down to a low balance before topping up again.
- **Tools that make you earn more, not just spend:** the **zones chaudes** heatmap shows where riders are right now; **"je rentre chez moi"** fills your trip home with paying rides; **favoris** turns one-time riders into regulars.

**One-line pitch:** *« Tu gardes l'essentiel de ta course, tu es payé en cash chaque jour, et tes clients reviennent vers toi. »*

## 4. Where to find drivers (the target list)

### A. Poach multi-app drivers — fastest supply 🥇
Drivers already on Yango/Heetch/local apps **already have the car, the papers, and the habit.** They lose nothing by adding a second app — and yours pays them more per ride. **Start here.** Find them at:
- Ride-app pickup hotspots: the **airport (Oum Tounsi)**, big hotels, **Marché Capitale**, shopping areas.
- Driver Facebook/WhatsApp groups (below).
- Just… order rides and pitch the driver in person at drop-off.

### B. Facebook — search these terms, join the groups
FB is where Nouakchott drivers organize. Search (FR + Arabic) and request to join:
- `Captain Nouakchott` · `Taxi Nouakchott` · `كابتنين نواكشوط` · `تاكسي نواكشوط`
- `Yango Nouakchott` / `Yango Captains Mauritanie` (multi-app drivers)
- `Covoiturage Nouakchott` · `VTC Mauritanie`
- `Vente voiture Nouakchott` / `سوق السيارات نواكشوط` (car owners = potential drivers)
- Neighborhood buy/sell groups for Tevragh Zeina, Ksar, Sebkha, Arafat
> Post D1 (below) in each, and DM active members. Keep a list of which groups convert.

### C. Physical spots — go in person with the flyer
Drivers respond to a real person + a printed flyer + a QR. Visit:
- **Taxi ranks / "garages"**: Marché Capitale, Carrefour BMD, Ksar, Stade Olympique, the airport queue.
- **Fuel stations** (Star Oil / Total / others) — drivers idle there; leave flyers, talk to a few.
- **Car washes** and mechanic streets — captive audience of car owners.

### D. Referral — every captain brings the next (see §6)
Your best recruiters are happy drivers. Turn it on from Day 3.

### E. Taxi unions / cooperatives — bulk onboarding (week 2+)
A single partnership with a taxi cooperative can onboard dozens at once. Slower to set up; high payoff. Note it for after the first wave.

## 6. Referral mechanics (driver → driver)

The cheapest, highest-trust supply you'll get.

- **Who:** any active captain refers another driver.
- **Trigger:** the referred driver passes KYC **and completes N rides** (suggest **20 rides within 14 days** — proves they're real and active, blocks abuse).
- **Reward:** bonus credited to the referrer's wallet (suggest **[set amount] MRU**), and a welcome bonus to the new driver too. Founders get a higher rate.
- **How to track (with manual onboarding):** when a new driver writes in, ask *"Qui t'a parlé d'Aloo ?"* and log the referrer in your tracker. Pay out when the ride count hits N.
- **Rider → rider** runs in parallel: referrer gets ride credit, new rider gets first ride free.

> Keep amounts simple and announce them clearly. Pay fast and publicly (a "merci [nom], bonus envoyé 🎉" message) — it fuels more referrals.

## 7. Onboarding & KYC checklist (run it the same way every time)

Documents to collect from each captain (from the app's `become-captain` flow):
- [ ] **CNI / NNI** (national ID)
- [ ] **Permis de conduire** (driver's license)
- [ ] **Carte grise** (vehicle registration)
- [ ] **Assurance** (insurance — check expiry)
- [ ] **Vignette** (road tax — check expiry)
- [ ] **Visite technique** (technical inspection — check expiry)
- [ ] **2+ photos of the vehicle** (front + plate)
- [ ] Phone number + name confirmed

Process:
1. Driver submits docs via WhatsApp (or in-app `Devenir Captain`).
2. Team reviews → approve/reject per document. **SLA: 24–72h** (faster = fewer drop-offs — aim same-day during launch).
3. On approval: driver gets login, a **window sticker + vest**, and a 2-min "how to go online / accept a ride / read the heatmap" walkthrough.
4. Set expiry reminders for assurance / vignette / visite technique (the app auto-suspends on expiry — warn them first).

## 8. The recruitment tracker (set this up before Day 1)

A simple sheet. One row per driver, move them right:

| Name | Phone | Source (group/spot/referrer) | Contacted | Interested | Docs received | KYC ✅ | Online | First ride | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Watch daily:** # contacted, # online, and where your best drivers came from — then double down on that source. **The number that matters is "online captains in the zone."** Everything else is upstream of it.
