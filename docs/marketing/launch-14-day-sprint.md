# Aloo — 14-Day Launch Sprint

> Goal: go from **nothing public** to **publicly live in Nouakchott** in two weeks, **without launching to empty streets**.
> Budget posture: **modest** (some ad money). Strategy: **drivers in week 1, riders in week 2.**
> Pair this with the [brand kit](./brand-kit.md). All copy below is ready to post.

⚠️ **The one risk with "ASAP":** if riders arrive before drivers, they open the app, see no cars, and never come back. So **week 1 is almost entirely about captains.** Don't run a single rider ad until the launch zone feels instant.

## Week 1 — SUPPLY (Days 1–7): get the cars

**Target by Day 7:** enough active captains in the launch zone that a rider would see a car **< 7 min away** at peak. For one dense set of neighborhoods, aim for a first wave of **30–60 verified captains** online.

| Day | Focus | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Accounts live | Finish pre-flight. Publish the *"coming soon"* post (P1) + the *driver recruitment* post (D1). Pin D1. |
| **2** | Recruit hard | Post D1 in every Nouakchott **taxi/driver Facebook group** + WhatsApp broadcast (D2 script). Visit 2–3 **fuel stations / taxi ranks** with flyers. |
| **3** | Onboard | Run KYC on first captains. Hand each one a **window sticker + vest**. Turn on the **driver referral bonus** (D3). |
| **4** | Proof + teaser | Film the **voice-booking demo** with a real captain (your hero asset). Post teaser P2. Keep recruiting. |
| **5** | Density check | Map where captains actually are. Fill gaps in the zone. Open a **rider waitlist** (free first ride) via P3. |
| **6** | Soft launch | Invite friends, family, and the drivers' own networks to ride **inside the zone only**. Seed real trips. |
| **7** | Tune | Measure pickup ETA & match rate. Fix dead spots. Collect 3–5 **testimonials / UGC** clips. |

## Modest budget — where it goes

Spend it **only after week 1** (no point boosting demand before cars exist). Allocate by ratio:

| Lever | Share | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook / Instagram ads** (Nouakchott, FR+AR) | ~40% | Boost P6 + P8, target the launch zone. Best reach-per-dirham here. |
| **TikTok boost + 2–3 micro-influencers** | ~25% | The voice demo is made for TikTok. Creators > polished ads. |
| **Local radio spot** (1 station, local language) | ~15% | Reaches households phones don't. Read the tagline + WhatsApp number. |
| **Printed materials** | ~10% | Window stickers + vests (rolling billboards) + QR flyers. |
| **Referral / free-ride credits** (both sides) | ~10% | The cheapest, highest-trust growth you have. |

**If budget is tight:** put 100% into driver stickers/vests + referral + boosting the single best TikTok. Organic + word-of-mouth carries the rest.

## Metrics — what "working" looks like

Track weekly (a simple spreadsheet is fine):

| Side | Metric | Healthy signal |
|---|---|---|
| Supply | Active captains in zone | growing toward your density target |
| Liquidity | **Pickup ETA** | trending **< 7 min** at peak |
| Liquidity | Requests with a car available | **> 90%** |
| Demand | New rider accounts / week | growing |
| Demand | Completed rides / week | growing |
| Demand | **W1 repeat rate** (riders who ride again within 7 days) | the real health metric — aim to push it up |
| Funnel | WhatsApp messages → accounts → first ride | watch where people drop |
| Unit | Rides / captain / day | drivers stay when this is healthy |

**Funnel bottleneck to watch:** every signup goes through your WhatsApp by hand. If messages pile up faster than you can onboard, riders churn before their first ride. Keep replies **under ~10 min** during launch; add a teammate or a simple waitlist form the moment it backs up.
