# Aloo — Voice Demo Video (hero asset)

> Your single most shareable asset. Voice booking is your #1 differentiator, and "say it, a car comes" is *made* for TikTok. **One shoot → three cuts.** Shot on a good phone is fine — authenticity beats polish here.

## The idea
Show the magic literally: a person **speaks**, a car **comes**. No typing, no app friction shown until the payoff. End on WhatsApp signup + first ride free.

## Specs
- **Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920.**
- **Subtitles always on** — 80%+ of people watch muted. Burn them in.
- Shoot the spoken booking line **at least three times — in Hassaniya, French, and one of pulaar/wolof/soninké** — so you can cut a version per audience; subtitle in FR + AR.
- Capture a **real screen recording** of the app's voice booking (don't fake the UI).
- Real captain, clean car, golden hour, a recognizable Nouakchott spot (Marché Capitale, a known street).

## Hook rules (first 1.5 seconds decide everything)
- Open on the **action or a bold line**, never a logo. Brand goes at the *end*.
- Hook line on screen: **« Ta voix suffit. »** / **« صوتك يكفي »** / **« sawtak kavi »** → thumb hovers → they speak. Pick whichever language fits the cut; subtitle the others.

## Production checklist
- [ ] Captain consent + clean, presentable car
- [ ] Golden-hour light; steady hands or a cheap phone gimbal
- [ ] Record **clean audio** of the spoken booking (Hassaniya + French + one of pulaar/wolof/soninké takes)
- [ ] **Screen-record the real app** voice booking + map tracking
- [ ] B-roll: car arriving, door, map screen, cash at drop-off, smiles, the mother scene
- [ ] Shoot vertical, leave headroom for burned-in subtitles (top & bottom safe zones)

## Sound
Warm, modern, light beat — or a **trending TikTok sound** (boosts reach). Keep VO minimal; let action + subtitles carry. Music low under any dialogue.

## End card (every version)
Logo (the "A") · **Parle. On t'amène.** / *احكي... ونوصّلوك.* / *tkellem · wnwasslouk* · **Crée ton compte sur WhatsApp +222 …** · **1ʳᵉ course offerte**
